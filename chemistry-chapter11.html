Chapter Overview

What is it?

This chapter details the composition of clean air, the steps involved in water purification, and the sources and effects of major air and water pollutants like carbon monoxide, sulfur dioxide, and greenhouse gases.

Why It Matters

Understanding environmental chemistry is critical for public health and climate science. Water purification ensures safe drinking water, while understanding pollution helps us mitigate climate change and acid rain.

03

Detailed Theory

01

Composition of Air & Water

Air: Clean, dry air consists of approximately 78% Nitrogen (N₂), 21% Oxygen (O₂), 0.9% Argon (Ar), and 0.04% Carbon Dioxide (CO₂).

Water: Pure water boils at exactly 100°C and freezes at 0°C. Natural water contains dissolved minerals and microbes, making it unsafe to drink without treatment.

02

Water Purification

To make water potable (safe to drink), it undergoes three main stages at a treatment plant:

  • 1. Sedimentation / Filtration: Large particles are allowed to settle, then water is filtered through sand and gravel to remove insoluble solids.
  • 2. Chlorination: Chlorine gas is added to kill bacteria and microbes. It is NOT filtered out; it remains to keep water safe in pipes.
03

Atmospheric Pollutants

  • Carbon Monoxide (CO): From incomplete combustion of fuels. Toxic, reduces oxygen transport in blood.
  • Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂): From burning coal. Causes acid rain, which kills aquatic life and damages buildings.
  • Oxides of Nitrogen (NOx): From car engines (high temperatures). Causes acid rain and photochemical smog.
  • Catalytic Converters: Found in car exhausts. Convert toxic CO and NOx into harmless CO₂ and N₂ using transition metal catalysts (Pt, Rh).

Worked Examples

Case Study 02 / Intermediate

Pollution Explanation

Question: Explain how sulfur dioxide causes acid rain, and state one consequence.

1. SO₂ dissolves in rainwater in the atmosphere.

2. Forms sulfurous/sulfuric acid, lowering the pH.

3. Falls as acid rain, killing fish in lakes and damaging limestone buildings.

Answer: SO₂ dissolves in rain to form acid, which lowers lake pH and kills aquatic life.

09

Laboratory Protocol

Experiment 01 / Percentage of Oxygen in Air

Iron Wool / Phosphorus

Method: Place wet iron wool in a graduated syringe. Leave for several days. The water level rises as oxygen is used up in rusting.

Exam Application: Examiners ask why the water rises. Answer: Oxygen is removed from the air by rusting iron, creating a partial vacuum that draws water up. Air shrinks by ~20%.

11

Error Database

Wrong

Water purification by distillation produces safe drinking water for large cities.

Correct

Distillation produces pure water (no minerals), but it is too expensive and energy-intensive for large-scale water treatment. Plants use filtration and chlorination.

Wrong

Carbon dioxide is the main cause of acid rain.

Correct

CO₂ forms weak carbonic acid, but acid rain (pH < 5.6) is primarily caused by sulfur dioxide (SO₂) and oxides of nitrogen (NOx).
