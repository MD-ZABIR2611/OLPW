Key Concepts

01

A competitive market has many firms, low barriers to entry and similar products.

02

A monopoly is a single seller with high barriers to entry.

03

Monopolies may charge higher prices and restrict output.

04

Monopolies can fund research and gain economies of scale.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
MonopolyA market with a single supplier and high barriers to entry.
Barriers to entryObstacles that make it difficult for new firms to enter a market.
Competitive marketA market with many buyers and sellers where no firm controls the price.

Practice Questions
