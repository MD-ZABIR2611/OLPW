SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Percentages & Proportion

A percentage represents a fraction out of 100. To increase a quantity by $R\%$, multiply it by $(1 + \frac{R}{100})$. To decrease, multiply by $(1 - \frac{R}{100})$.

Interest Types

Simple Interest: Calculated only on the initial principal amount.

Compound Interest: Calculated on the principal AND the accumulated interest from previous periods. This causes the amount to grow exponentially.

Ratio & Proportion

A ratio compares two or more quantities. To share an amount $A$ in the ratio $m:n$, find the total number of parts ($m+n$), divide $A$ by the total parts to find the value of one part, then multiply by $m$ and $n$.

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

DIRECT PROPORTION: Two quantities increase or decrease at the same rate. If $y \propto x$, then $y = kx$.

INVERSE PROPORTION: As one quantity increases, the other decreases. If $y \propto \frac{1}{x}$, then $y = \frac{k}{x}$.

SPEED: The rate of change of distance with respect to time.

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Reverse Percentage Calculation

A car costs $\$21,600$ after a 10% sales tax is added. What was the original price before tax?

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Represent final value 110% = $\$21,600$ Original is 100%, +10% tax = 110%
2. Find 1% 1% = $21600 \div 110 = 196.36$ Divide by 110
3. Find 100% (Original) 100% = $196.36 \times 100 = \$19,636.36$ Multiply by 100
4. Final Answer $\$19,636.36$ Round to 2 decimal places for currency
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Identifying Reverse Percentages

Watch for keywords like "original value", "before the increase", or "ex-VAT". Never just subtract 10% from the final amount—this is a trap! The 10% was calculated on the *original* amount, not the final amount.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Calculating compound interest by simply multiplying the simple interest by the number of years.

Correction: You must recalculate the interest for each year on the new total. Use the formula $A = P(1 + \frac{r}{100})^n$.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • Compound Interest: $A = P(1 + \frac{R}{100})^n$
  • Speed = Distance ÷ Time
  • Direct Proportion: $y = kx$. Inverse Proportion: $y = \frac{k}{x}$
  • Always find the "value of 1 part" when solving ratio questions.
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

Divide $\$840$ between Alice and Bob in the ratio $3:4$.

3 marks:

1. Total parts = $3 + 4 = 7$

2. Value of 1 part = $840 \div 7 = \$120$

3. Alice = $3 \times 120 = \$360$. Bob = $4 \times 120 = \$480$.
