Key Concepts

01

The income statement shows revenue, costs and profit over a period.

02

The statement of financial position shows assets, liabilities and equity at a date.

03

Profitability ratios include gross and net profit margins.

04

Liquidity ratios include the current ratio and acid test ratio.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Gross profit marginGross profit as a percentage of revenue.
Acid test ratio(Current assets - inventory) / current liabilities.
Retained profitProfit kept in the business after tax and dividends.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Revenue is $200,000 and gross profit $80,000. Calculate the gross margin.

Model answer80,000 / 200,000 x 100 = 40%.
Application

2. Why might a bank look at the current ratio?

Model answerTo judge whether the business can pay its short-term debts before lending.
