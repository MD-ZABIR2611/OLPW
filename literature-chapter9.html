Key Concepts

Plays are written to be performed.

02

Stage directions guide actors and set the scene.

Dramatic irony occurs when the audience knows more than a character.

04

Soliloquies reveal a character's inner thoughts.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
SoliloquyA speech in which a character speaks their thoughts alone on stage.
Dramatic ironyWhen the audience knows something a character does not.
AsideA brief remark to the audience that other characters do not hear.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Refer to the 'audience' rather than the 'reader' when writing about drama.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. What is a soliloquy?

Model answerA speech where a character alone on stage reveals their inner thoughts.
Application

2. How does dramatic irony create tension?

Model answerThe audience anticipates consequences that the character cannot see.
