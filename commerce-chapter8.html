Key Concepts

01

Main modes are road, rail, sea, air and pipeline.

02

Choice depends on cost, speed, distance, nature of goods and safety.

03

Containerisation speeds up loading and reduces theft.

04

Air transport suits valuable and perishable goods.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
ContainerisationTransporting goods in standard-sized sealed containers.
FreightGoods carried by a means of transport, or the charge for carrying them.
Perishable goodsGoods that decay quickly, such as fresh fruit and flowers.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Always justify your choice of transport with at least two factors from the scenario.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Which mode of transport suits fresh flowers exported abroad? Why?

Model answerAir transport, because it is fast and the flowers are perishable and high in value.
Application

2. Give two advantages of containerisation.

Model answerFaster loading and reduced damage and theft.
