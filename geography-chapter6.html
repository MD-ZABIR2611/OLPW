Key Concepts

01

Destructive waves erode; constructive waves deposit.

02

Erosional landforms include cliffs, caves, arches and stacks.

03

Longshore drift transports sediment along the coast.

04

Coasts can be protected by hard or soft engineering.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Longshore driftThe movement of sediment along a coast by waves.
SpitA ridge of sand extending from the coast into the sea.
Hard engineeringMan-made structures such as sea walls to control coastal processes.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Describe the sequence cave, arch, stack, stump.

Model answerErosion widens cracks into caves, caves break through to form arches, arches collapse to leave stacks, which erode to stumps.
Application

2. Give one advantage of beach nourishment.

Model answerIt looks natural and supports tourism.
