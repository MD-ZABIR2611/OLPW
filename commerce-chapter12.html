Key Concepts

01

Commercial banks accept deposits, lend money and provide payment services.

02

Payment methods include cash, cheques, debit and credit cards and online transfer.

03

The central bank issues currency and supervises commercial banks.

04

Loans and overdrafts provide finance to businesses.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Current accountA bank account used for day-to-day payments.
OverdraftPermission to withdraw more money than is in a current account, up to a limit.
Standing orderAn instruction to a bank to pay a fixed amount regularly.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Give two services commercial banks offer businesses.

Model answerLoans and overdrafts, and payment services such as online transfers.
Application

2. What is the difference between a debit card and a credit card?

Model answerA debit card takes money directly from a bank account; a credit card borrows money that is repaid later.
