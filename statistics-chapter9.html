Key Concepts

01

Range = largest − smallest value.

02

Variance measures the average squared deviation from the mean.

03

Standard deviation is the square root of variance.

04

A low standard deviation means data is consistent.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Standard deviationA measure of how spread out values are around the mean.
VarianceThe mean of the squared deviations from the mean.
RangeThe difference between the highest and lowest values.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

When comparing data sets, comment on both an average and a measure of spread.

Practice Questions
