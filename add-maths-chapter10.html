Key Concepts

01

n! = n × (n − 1) × ... × 1.

02

Permutations count arrangements where order matters: ⁿPᵣ = n!/(n − r)!.

03

Combinations count selections where order does not matter: ⁿCᵣ = n!/(r!(n − r)!).

04

Restrictions are handled by treating grouped items as one unit.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
PermutationAn arrangement of items in which order matters.
CombinationA selection of items in which order does not matter.
FactorialThe product of all positive integers up to n.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Ask 'does order matter?' to decide between permutations and combinations.
