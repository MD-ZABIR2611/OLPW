End-of-year adjustments to final accounts.

Key Concepts

01

Accrued expenses are expenses owed at the year end.

02

Prepaid expenses are paid in advance for the next period.

03

Depreciation spreads the cost of a non-current asset over its useful life.

04

Straight-line and reducing-balance are the two main depreciation methods.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Accrued expenseAn expense incurred in the period but not yet paid at the year end.
PrepaymentAn expense paid in advance that relates to a future period.
DepreciationThe loss in value of a non-current asset over time.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Accrued expenses are current liabilities; prepayments are current assets. Place them correctly on the statement of financial position.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. A machine costs $10,000, has a life of 5 years and no residual value. Calculate straight-line depreciation.

Model answer10,000 / 5 = $2,000 per year.
Application

2. Rent paid is $5,000 but $500 relates to next year. What is the rent expense?

Model answer$4,500, with a $500 prepayment shown as a current asset.
