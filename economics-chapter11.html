Key Concepts

01

Main aims: economic growth, low unemployment, stable prices and a balance of payments stability.

02

Fiscal policy uses taxation and government spending.

03

Monetary policy changes interest rates and the money supply.

04

Supply-side policies raise productive capacity, for example through education and training.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Fiscal policyChanges in government spending and taxation to influence the economy.
Monetary policyChanges in interest rates and the money supply to influence the economy.
Progressive taxA tax where the percentage paid rises as income rises.

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Explain the chain of effects: policy change, then spending, then output, jobs and prices. Missing links cost marks.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. How could lower interest rates reduce unemployment?

Model answerBorrowing becomes cheaper, so households spend more and firms invest more; higher demand leads firms to hire more workers.
Application

2. Give one example of a supply-side policy.

Model answerSpending more on education and training to increase the skills and productivity of the labour force.
