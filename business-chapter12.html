Key Concepts

01

Internal finance includes retained profit and sale of assets.

02

External finance includes loans, overdrafts, share issues and leasing.

03

A cash-flow forecast predicts inflows and outflows.

04

Working capital = current assets - current liabilities.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Cash-flow forecastAn estimate of future cash inflows and outflows over a period.
OverdraftA flexible short-term loan allowing a business to spend more than it has in its account.
Working capitalCurrent assets minus current liabilities.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Match the source of finance to the purpose: short-term needs use overdrafts, long-term assets use loans or share capital.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Why is profit not the same as cash?

Model answerSales on credit and purchases of assets affect cash and profit at different times.
Application

2. Give two ways to improve cash flow.

Model answerChase customers for faster payment and negotiate longer credit terms with suppliers.
