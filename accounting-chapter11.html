Irrecoverable Debts and Provisions

Writing off bad debts and allowing for doubtful debts.

04Key concepts

—Your status
01

Key Concepts

01

An irrecoverable debt is written off as an expense.

02

A provision for doubtful debts reflects expected non-payment.

03

Increases in the provision reduce profit.

04

Trade receivables are shown net of the provision.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Irrecoverable debtA debt that will not be paid and is written off as an expense.
Provision for doubtful debtsAn estimate of trade receivables that may not be paid.
Net realisable valueThe amount expected to be received after deducting costs of sale.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Only the change in the provision goes to the income statement; the full provision is deducted from receivables.
