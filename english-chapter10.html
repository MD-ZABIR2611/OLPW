Key Concepts

01

Apostrophes show possession or omission, never plurals.

02

Semicolons link closely related main clauses.

03

Colons introduce lists, explanations or quotations.

04

Speech marks enclose the exact words spoken.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Apostrophe of omissionAn apostrophe that shows letters have been left out, as in 'don't'.
SemicolonA punctuation mark linking two closely related independent clauses.
ColonA punctuation mark used to introduce a list, explanation or example.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Leave five minutes at the end to proofread; accuracy is a large part of the writing mark.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Correct: 'The dog wagged it's tail.'

Model answerThe dog wagged its tail. (its = belonging to it; it's = it is.)
Application

2. Punctuate: I need three things bread milk and eggs

Model answerI need three things: bread, milk and eggs.
