Key Concepts

01

The business entity principle separates the owner from the business.

02

The accruals principle matches income and expenses to the period they relate to.

03

Prudence avoids overstating profits or assets.

04

Consistency requires the same methods to be used each year.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Accruals principleIncome and expenses are recorded in the period they are earned or incurred, not when cash moves.
PrudenceProfits and assets should not be overstated, and losses should be recognised when expected.
Going concernThe assumption that a business will continue trading for the foreseeable future.

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Which principle explains why the owner's private car is not a business asset?

Model answerThe business entity principle.
Application

2. Explain the consistency principle.

Model answerThe same accounting methods should be used from year to year so that results can be compared.
