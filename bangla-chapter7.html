Key Concepts

01

Sentences are simple (সরল), complex (জটিল) or compound (যৌগিক).

02

Sentences can be affirmative, negative, interrogative or exclamatory.

03

Transformation changes structure without changing meaning.

04

Word order is usually subject-object-verb.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
