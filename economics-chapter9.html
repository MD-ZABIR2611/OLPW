Key Concepts

01

Firms are classified by sector: primary, secondary and tertiary.

02

Total cost = fixed costs + variable costs.

03

Economies of scale lower average costs as a firm grows.

04

Profit = total revenue - total cost; firms may also aim for growth or survival.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Fixed costsCosts that do not change with the level of output, such as rent.
Economies of scaleFalls in average cost as the scale of production increases.
Average costTotal cost divided by the quantity produced.

Exam Tip

Examiner says

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. A firm has fixed costs of $500 and variable costs of $2 per unit. What is the total cost of 100 units?

Model answerTotal cost = 500 + (2 x 100) = $700.
Application

2. Give one example of an internal economy of scale.

Model answerBulk buying: a large firm can buy raw materials in large quantities at a lower price per unit.
