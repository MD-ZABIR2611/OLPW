Transformations of Mean and Standard Deviation

Coded and combined data appear constantly in 4040. Multiplying by 3 multiplies the spread by 3, but adding 5 merely shifts every value without changing the spread.

Key Concepts

01

If every value is transformed to y = ax + b, then the new mean is a×(old mean) + b and the new standard deviation is |a|×(old standard deviation).

02

Adding a constant shifts the mean but leaves the standard deviation and variance unchanged because distances between values do not change.

03

Multiplying by a constant multiplies the standard deviation by its absolute value and the variance by its square.

04

For combined data from two groups, the overall mean is the weighted mean (n₁x̄₁ + n₂x̄₂)/(n₁ + n₂), not the simple average of the two means.

05

The total of a set of values is Σx = n×x̄, the key link between summary statistics and raw sums.

06

Coding with y = (x − a)/b simplifies awkward means before reversing the transformation to return to the original scale.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Linear transformationA change of scale of the form y = ax + b applied to every value in a data set, shifting and rescaling the distribution.
CodingThe use of a simple transformation such as y = (x − a)/b to make calculation of the mean and standard deviation easier.
MeanThe arithmetic average of a set of values, equal to the sum of the values divided by the number of values.
VarianceThe mean squared deviation from the mean, measuring spread in squared units, with s² = Σ(x − x̄)²/n for raw data.
Standard deviationThe positive square root of the variance, measuring spread in the same units as the data.
Sum of valuesThe total Σx of all values in a set, recovered from the mean by multiplying the mean by the number of values.
Deviation from the meanThe difference x − x̄ between a value and the mean, used in the definition of variance and standard deviation.
Combined data setTwo or more groups merged into one, whose total mean and standard deviation must be recomputed from the raw sums of each group.
03

Exam Tip

Exam tip

Before combining two classes, write Σx = n×x̄ for each group separately; adding constants never changes the standard deviation, multiplying always does.

04

Practice Questions

Structured practice — write your answer, then reveal the model.

FOUNDATION

A data set has mean 8 and standard deviation 2. Each value x is transformed to y = 3x + 5. Find the new mean and the new standard deviation.

Model answerNew mean = 3×8 + 5 = 24 + 5 = 29 (1). Multiplying by 3 multiplies the standard deviation by 3, and adding 5 does not change it (1). New standard deviation = 3×2 = 6 (1).
CHALLENGE

Class A has 20 students with mean mark 64; class B has 30 students with mean mark 76. Find the mean mark of the combined group of 50 students and explain why it is not 70.

Model answerTotal for A = 20×64 = 1280 and total for B = 30×76 = 2280 (1). Combined total = 1280 + 2280 = 3560 (1). Combined mean = 3560/50 = 71.2 (1). It is not 70 because the groups have different sizes; the mean is weighted towards class B, which has more students and the higher mean (1).
