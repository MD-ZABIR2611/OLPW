Key Concepts

01

∫xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹/(n + 1) + c, for n ≠ −1.

02

Definite integrals give the area between a curve and the x-axis.

03

Velocity is the derivative of displacement; acceleration is the derivative of velocity.

04

Integrating acceleration gives velocity; integrating velocity gives displacement.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
IntegrationThe reverse process of differentiation.
Definite integralAn integral with limits that gives a numerical value.
KinematicsThe study of motion using displacement, velocity and acceleration.
