Partnerships and Limited Companies

Accounts for businesses with more than one owner.

04Key concepts

Key Concepts

01

A partnership agreement sets out profit-sharing ratios, interest and salaries.

02

The appropriation account shares profit between partners.

03

Limited companies raise capital by issuing shares.

04

Shareholders have limited liability; companies pay dividends.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Appropriation accountAn account showing how profit is shared between partners.
Limited liabilityOwners can only lose the amount they have invested.
DividendThe share of profit paid to shareholders.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

In appropriation accounts, deduct interest on capital and partners' salaries before sharing the residual profit.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Profit is $30,000, shared 2:1 between A and B. How much does A receive?

Model answer30,000 x 2/3 = $20,000.
Application

2. State one advantage of forming a limited company.

Model answerShareholders have limited liability, protecting their personal assets.
