Key Concepts

01

Business units include sole traders, partnerships, companies and cooperatives.

02

Public corporations are owned by the state.

03

The stock exchange is a market for buying and selling second-hand shares.

04

Shares and debentures are ways companies raise long-term finance.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Stock exchangeA market where existing shares and securities are bought and sold.
DebentureA long-term loan to a company that pays fixed interest.
CooperativeA business owned and run by its members, who share the profits.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Explain how the stock exchange benefits both companies (raising capital) and investors (easy buying and selling).

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. State two functions of the stock exchange.

Model answerIt provides a market for second-hand shares and helps companies raise capital.
Application

2. Distinguish between a share and a debenture.

Model answerA share gives part-ownership and a dividend; a debenture is a loan with fixed interest and no ownership.
