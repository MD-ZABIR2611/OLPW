Motion

CORE EXTENDED
THEORY

Kinematics & Graphs

An object is in motion if its position changes relative to a fixed point. Uniform motion occurs at a constant velocity. Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity. Free fall is a specific type of acceleration where air resistance is ignored, and the object accelerates at gravity (9.8 m/s²). Terminal velocity is reached when the drag force equals gravitational force, resulting in zero acceleration.

DEFINITIONS

Key Terms

Displacement: Distance travelled in a specific direction.

Velocity: Speed in a given direction.

Terminal Velocity: The constant maximum velocity reached by an object when the drag force equals the driving force.

FORMULAE

Equations of Motion

speed (v) = distance (d) / time (t)
acceleration (a) = change in velocity (Δv) / time (t)
VISUALS / GRAPHS

Motion Graphs

The gradient of a distance-time graph represents speed. The gradient of a speed-time graph represents acceleration. The area under a speed-time graph represents total distance travelled.

EXAM PRACTICE

Worked Example

Question 1 (Extended)

A car accelerates from 10 m/s to 30 m/s in 5 seconds. Calculate its acceleration.

Formula: a = (v - u) / t

Substitution: a = (30 - 10) / 5

Answer: a = 4 m/s²
