Key Concepts

01

Gross profit = revenue - cost of sales.

02

Profit for the year = gross profit + other income - expenses.

03

The statement of financial position lists assets, liabilities and capital.

04

Capital at the end = opening capital + profit - drawings.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Gross profitRevenue minus the cost of sales.
Cost of salesOpening inventory + purchases - closing inventory.
DrawingsResources taken from the business by the owner for private use.

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Use proper headings with the business name, the statement title and the date or period. Presentation marks are easy to earn.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Opening inventory $4,000, purchases $30,000, closing inventory $6,000. Calculate cost of sales.

Model answer4,000 + 30,000 - 6,000 = $28,000.
Application

2. Where are drawings shown?

Model answerDeducted from capital on the statement of financial position.
