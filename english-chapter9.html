Key Concepts

01

Simple sentences have one clause; compound sentences join clauses with conjunctions.

02

Complex sentences include a subordinate clause.

03

Subject and verb must agree.

04

Avoid comma splices by using full stops, semicolons or conjunctions.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
ClauseA group of words containing a subject and a verb.
Subordinate clauseA clause that cannot stand alone as a sentence.
Comma spliceJoining two main clauses with only a comma.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Vary your openings: start some sentences with a verb, an adverb or a subordinate clause.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Correct: 'It was raining, we stayed inside.'

Model answerIt was raining, so we stayed inside. (Or: It was raining; we stayed inside.)
Application

2. Write a complex sentence about a storm.

Model answerAlthough the storm had passed, the streets were still flooded.
