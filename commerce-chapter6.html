Key Concepts

01

Countries trade because resources, climate and skills differ.

02

Visible trade is goods; invisible trade is services.

03

The balance of trade compares visible exports and imports.

04

Tariffs, quotas and embargoes restrict trade.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Visible tradeThe import and export of physical goods.
Invisible tradeThe import and export of services such as tourism and insurance.
QuotaA limit on the quantity of a good that can be imported.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Always state whether the balance is favourable (surplus) or unfavourable (deficit) when interpreting data.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Distinguish between visible and invisible exports.

Model answerVisible exports are goods sold abroad; invisible exports are services sold to foreigners, such as tourism.
Application

2. Give two reasons for international trade.

Model answerCountries cannot produce everything they need, and trade allows specialisation and lower costs.
