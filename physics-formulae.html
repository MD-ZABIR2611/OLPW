DEFINITIONS & FORMULAE

Key Definitions

Speed

Distance travelled per unit time.

Acceleration

Change in velocity per unit time.

Force

A push or pull that causes a change in shape or motion.

Density

Mass per unit volume.

Momentum

The product of mass and velocity.

Core Formulae
