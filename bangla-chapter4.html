Samas (Compound Words)

সমাস: forming compound words.

04Key concepts
03Key terms
02Practice questions
—Your status
01

Key Concepts

01

Samas joins two or more words into one.

02

Main types include দ্বন্দ্ব, তৎপুরুষ, বহুব্রীহি, কর্মধারয় and দ্বিগু.

03

Example: মা ও বাবা = মা-বাবা (দ্বন্দ্ব).

04

Identifying samas requires breaking it into its parts.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
সমাসCombining words to form a compound.
দ্বন্দ্ব সমাসA compound where both parts are equally important.
দ্বিগু সমাসA compound starting with a number.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Write the ব্যাসবাক্য (expanded form) first to identify the samas correctly.
