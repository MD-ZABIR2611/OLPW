Workers and Trade Unions

How wages are set and what trade unions do.

Key Concepts

01

Wages are determined by the demand for and supply of labour.

02

Skilled workers usually earn more because their supply is limited and their productivity is high.

03

Trade unions bargain collectively for better pay and conditions.

04

Division of labour raises output but can make work repetitive.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Trade unionAn organisation of workers that aims to protect and improve members' pay and working conditions.
Collective bargainingNegotiation between a trade union and employers on behalf of a group of workers.
Division of labourSplitting production into separate tasks performed by different workers.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

For wage-difference questions, use at least two factors such as skills, qualifications, danger and demand for the product.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. State two reasons why doctors earn more than cleaners.

Model answerDoctors need long training so supply is low, and they have high productivity and responsibility.
Application

2. Explain one way a trade union can increase wages.

Model answerThrough collective bargaining, backed if necessary by industrial action such as a strike, it can pressure employers to raise pay.
