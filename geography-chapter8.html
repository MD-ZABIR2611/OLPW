Key Concepts

01

Climate is the average weather over about 30 years.

02

Equatorial climates are hot and wet all year.

03

Hot deserts have very low rainfall and large daily temperature ranges.

04

Plants and animals adapt to their climates.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
ClimateThe average weather conditions over a long period.
Tropical rainforestA dense forest in hot, wet equatorial regions.
AdaptationA feature that helps an organism survive in its environment.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Link vegetation adaptations directly to climatic features, such as drip tips for heavy rain.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Why do deserts have large daily temperature ranges?

Model answerClear skies allow strong heating by day and rapid heat loss at night.
Application

2. Give one adaptation of rainforest trees.

Model answerButtress roots to support tall trees in shallow soils.
