Key Concepts

01

Cumulative frequency is a running total.

02

Plot at upper class boundaries.

03

The median is at n/2; quartiles at n/4 and 3n/4.

04

Box plots show minimum, quartiles, median and maximum.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
MedianThe middle value of ordered data.
Interquartile rangeUpper quartile minus lower quartile.
PercentileA value below which a given percentage of data falls.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

The IQR measures spread of the middle 50% and is not affected by outliers.
