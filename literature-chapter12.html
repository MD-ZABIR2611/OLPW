Key Concepts

01

Plot follows exposition, rising action, climax, falling action and resolution.

02

Writers may use flashbacks or multiple timelines.

03

Cliffhangers create suspense.

04

Endings may be closed or open.

Key Terms

TermExam definition
ExpositionThe introduction of characters, setting and situation.
ResolutionThe part of a story where conflicts are resolved.
Cyclical structureA structure where the ending mirrors the beginning.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Comment on why an event happens at a particular point, not just what happens.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. What is a cyclical structure?

Model answerA structure where the text ends where it began, often suggesting nothing has changed.
Application

2. What is an open ending?

Model answerAn ending that leaves questions unanswered for the reader to consider.
