Geometry & Angles

CORE
SECTION 01 — CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Shapes, Lines & Bearings

Geometry is the study of shapes, sizes, and the properties of space. This chapter covers the fundamental rules of angles, from basic intersecting lines to polygons and parallel lines, concluding with bearings and geometric constructions.

SECTION 02 — LEARNING OBJECTIVES

Chapter Goals

  • Calculate angles on straight lines and around points.
  • Apply alternate, corresponding, and co-interior angle rules.
  • Calculate interior and exterior angles of polygons.
  • Understand congruence and similarity.
  • Measure and calculate three-figure bearings.
SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Angle Rules

Angles on a straight line add up to $180^\circ$. Angles around a point add up to $360^\circ$. Vertically opposite angles are equal.

Polygons

The sum of interior angles of an $n$-sided polygon is $(n-2) \times 180^\circ$.

The sum of exterior angles of ANY polygon is $360^\circ$.

For a regular polygon, each interior angle + each exterior angle = $180^\circ$.

Bearings

A bearing is a direction measured from North, clockwise, and written as a three-digit number (e.g., $065^\circ$, not $65^\circ$).

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

POLYGON: A closed 2D shape with straight sides.

CONGRUENT: Shapes that are identical in size and shape.

SIMILAR: Shapes that have the same angles but different sizes (proportional sides).

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Polygon Angle Calculation

Find the size of each interior angle in a regular octagon (8 sides).

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Sum of interior angles $(8-2) \times 180 = 6 \times 180 = 1080^\circ$ Formula: $(n-2) \times 180$
2. Find one angle (regular) $1080 \div 8 = 135^\circ$ Divide total by number of sides
3. Final Answer $135^\circ$ Each interior angle
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Geometric Reasoning

When calculating angles, always state the angle fact you are using. E.g., "Angle $x = 65^\circ$ because alternate angles in parallel lines are equal." Do not just write the number.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Writing bearings as 2-digit numbers (e.g., $45^\circ$).

Correction: Bearings must always be 3 digits. Pad with a zero: $045^\circ$.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • Interior sum of polygon: $(n-2) \times 180^\circ$.
  • Exterior sum of ANY polygon: $360^\circ$.
  • Alternate angles are equal. Corresponding angles are equal. Co-interior angles sum to $180^\circ$.
  • Bearings: 3 digits, clockwise from North.
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

The bearing of $B$ from $A$ is $110^\circ$. Find the bearing of $A$ from $B$.

2 marks:

1. Add $180^\circ$ to the original bearing (if less than $180^\circ$): $110 + 180 = 290$.

Answer: $290^\circ$
