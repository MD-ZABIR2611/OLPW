Key Concepts

01

Hadith record the sayings, actions and approvals of the Prophet.

02

A hadith has an isnad (chain) and matn (text).

03

The six major collections include Bukhari and Muslim.

04

Hadith are graded as sahih, hasan or da'if.

Key Terms

TermExam definition
IsnadThe chain of narrators of a hadith.
MatnThe text of a hadith.
SahihAn authentic, sound hadith.
