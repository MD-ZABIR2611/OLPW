What influences how households use their income.

Key Concepts

01

Income is the main influence on spending; as income rises, spending rises but at a slower rate.

02

Higher interest rates encourage saving and discourage borrowing.

03

Confidence about the future affects how much households spend.

04

Poorer households spend a larger proportion of their income on necessities.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Disposable incomeIncome left after direct taxes have been paid and benefits received.
SavingThe part of disposable income that is not spent.
Consumer confidenceHow optimistic households feel about their future income and the economy.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

When asked why spending patterns differ, compare income groups and refer to age, family size and interest rates.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Give two reasons why a household may save more.

Model answerHigher interest rates make saving more rewarding, and uncertainty about future jobs encourages precautionary saving.
Application

2. Why do low-income households spend a higher proportion on food?

Model answerFood is a necessity, so it takes up a large share of a small income, leaving little for saving or luxuries.
