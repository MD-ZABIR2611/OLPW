Statistics & Data Handling

CORE
SECTION 01 — CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Collecting & Interpreting Data

Statistics involves collecting, organizing, analyzing, and presenting data. This chapter covers calculating averages (mean, median, mode), measuring spread (range, quartiles), and interpreting various statistical graphs like histograms and cumulative frequency curves.

SECTION 02 — LEARNING OBJECTIVES

Chapter Goals

  • Calculate mean, median, mode, and range from raw data.
  • Calculate averages from frequency tables.
  • Draw and interpret histograms and cumulative frequency diagrams.
  • Find quartiles and interquartile range (IQR).
SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Averages & Spread

The three main averages are:

1. Mean: Total sum of data $\div$ number of items.

2. Median: The middle value when data is ordered.

3. Mode: The most frequent value.

Quartiles & IQR

The Lower Quartile ($Q_1$) is the value 1/4 of the way through the data. The Upper Quartile ($Q_3$) is 3/4 of the way through.

Interquartile Range (IQR): $IQR = Q_3 - Q_1$. This measures the spread of the middle 50% of the data.

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

POPULATION: The entire group of items or individuals being studied.

SAMPLE: A smaller group taken from the population for analysis.

HISTOGRAM: A chart showing frequency density against class interval. Bars are touching, and area represents frequency.

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Mean from Frequency Table

Calculate the mean number of pets from the data below.

Pets ($x$) Frequency ($f$) $f \times x$
0 10 0
1 8 8
2 5 10
Totals 23 ($\sum f$) 18 ($\sum fx$)

Mean = $\frac{\sum fx}{\sum f} = \frac{18}{23} = 0.78$ (2 d.p.)

SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Cumulative Frequency

When drawing a cumulative frequency curve, plot the point at the upper end of each class interval, not the middle. Join points with a smooth curve. To find the median, draw a horizontal line from $y = n/2$ to the curve, then drop down to the x-axis.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Using frequency as the height of a bar in a histogram.

Correction: Histograms plot Frequency DENSITY on the y-axis, not frequency. Area = Frequency.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • Mean = Sum $\div$ Count.
  • Median = Middle value.
  • Mode = Most frequent.
  • IQR = $Q_3 - Q_1$.
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

The mean of 5 numbers is 12. Four of the numbers are 10, 8, 15, and 9. Find the 5th number.

2 marks:

1. Total sum = $5 \times 12 = 60$.

2. Sum of 4 numbers = $10 + 8 + 15 + 9 = 42$.

3. 5th number = $60 - 42 = 18$.
