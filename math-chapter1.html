SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Number Classification & Indices

Natural numbers are positive integers (1, 2, 3...). Integers include negative numbers and zero. Rational numbers can be written as fractions (p/q). Irrational numbers cannot (e.g., $\pi$, $\sqrt{2}$).

Laws of Indices

1. Multiplication: $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$

2. Division: $a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}$

3. Power of a power: $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$

4. Zero power: $a^0 = 1$

5. Negative indices: $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

PRIME NUMBER: A number with exactly two factors: 1 and itself.

STANDARD FORM: A number written as $A \times 10^n$, where $1 \le A < 10$ and $n$ is an integer.

BOUNDS: The limits of accuracy for a rounded measurement. The lower bound is the smallest value that would round up to the given number; the upper bound is the largest value that would round down.

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Standard Form Calculation

Calculate $(3 \times 10^4) \times (2 \times 10^5)$. Give your answer in standard form.

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Group numbers $(3 \times 2) \times (10^4 \times 10^5)$ Use commutative law
2. Multiply coefficients $6 \times (10^4 \times 10^5)$ $3 \times 2 = 6$
3. Apply index law $6 \times 10^{4+5}$ $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$
4. Final Answer $6 \times 10^9$ Already in standard form ($1 \le 6 < 10$)
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Bounds & Accuracy

When a question asks for "bounds", always write your answer as an inequality: $LB \le x < UB$. For example, if a length is 5cm to the nearest cm, the bounds are $4.5 \text{ cm} \le x < 5.5 \text{ cm}$.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Writing $10^0 = 0$.

Correction: Any number to the power of 0 is 1. $10^0 = 1$.

Mistake: Forgetting to adjust standard form if the coefficient is 10 or greater. E.g., writing $12 \times 10^5$. It must be $1.2 \times 10^6$.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • $a^{-n} = 1 / a^n$.
  • 1 is NOT a prime number.
  • Standard form: $A \times 10^n$ where $1 \le A < 10$.
  • Upper bound = rounded value + half the precision.
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

A number $x$ is rounded to 2 significant figures. The result is 340. Write down the error interval for $x$.

2 marks:

1. 340 to 2 sig figs means the precision is to the nearest 10.

2. Half of 10 is 5.

Lower bound = $340 - 5 = 335$.

Upper bound = $340 + 5 = 345$.

Answer: $335 \le x < 345$
