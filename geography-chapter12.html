Key Concepts

01

Industry is primary, secondary, tertiary or quaternary.

02

Location factors include raw materials, labour, markets and transport.

03

High-technology industries cluster near universities.

04

Industry can cause pollution and environmental damage.

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Secondary industryManufacturing goods from raw materials.
Footloose industryAn industry not tied to a particular location.
Industrial estateAn area planned for factories and businesses.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. What is a footloose industry?

Model answerAn industry that can locate almost anywhere because it is not tied to raw materials.
Application

2. Give two factors affecting industrial location.

Model answerAccess to markets and a supply of labour.
