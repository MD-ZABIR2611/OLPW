Key Concepts

01

Informative advertising gives facts; persuasive advertising encourages buying.

02

Media include television, radio, newspapers, posters and the internet.

03

Advertising can increase sales and help new products enter a market.

04

Advertising may raise costs and mislead consumers.

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Persuasive advertisingAdvertising that tries to convince consumers to buy a product.
Advertising mediumThe channel used to deliver an advert, such as TV or social media.
Brand imageThe impression consumers have of a product or business.
