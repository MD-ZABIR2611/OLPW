SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Problem Solving Strategy

When faced with a complex problem, do not panic. Follow this sequence:

1. READ: Highlight key numbers and words.

2. IDENTIFY: What topic is this? (e.g., trigonometry, algebra?). What formula is needed?

3. SOLVE: Write down the formula. Substitute. Calculate.

4. CHECK: Does the answer make sense? Check units.

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

MULTI-STEP PROBLEM: A question that requires applying more than one mathematical concept or formula to reach the final answer.

PROOF: A logical mathematical argument that shows a statement is true.

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Multi-Step Problem

A cylindrical water tank has a diameter of $1.4\text{ m}$ and a height of $2\text{ m}$. Water flows into it at $50\text{ litres/min}$. How long will it take to fill the tank? ($1\text{ m}^3 = 1000\text{ litres}$)

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Find radius $r = 1.4 / 2 = 0.7\text{ m}$ Radius is half diameter
2. Volume of cylinder $V = \pi r^2 h = \pi \times 0.7^2 \times 2 = 0.98\pi \text{ m}^3$ Use $\pi$ button
3. Convert to litres $0.98\pi \times 1000 = 3078.76\text{ litres}$ $1\text{ m}^3 = 1000\text{ L}$
4. Time = Vol / Rate $3078.76 / 50 = 61.57\text{ mins}$ Divide by flow rate
5. Final Answer $61.6\text{ minutes}$ (1 d.p.) Round for final answer
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Time Management

Allocate roughly 1 minute per mark. If a question is worth 4 marks, spend no more than 4 minutes on it. If you are stuck, move on and return to it later. Always write down your formulas, as method marks are awarded even if the final calculation is wrong.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Rounding too early in a multi-step problem.

Correction: Keep exact values in your calculator (or use memory functions) until the very last step. Only round the final answer.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • Always check units (cm vs m, litres vs m³).
  • Write down formulas before substituting.
  • Keep exact values until the final step.
  • 1 minute per mark.
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Challenge)

The area of a circle is $144\pi \text{ cm}^2$. Calculate the circumference of the circle.

3 marks:

1. $\pi r^2 = 144\pi \Rightarrow r^2 = 144 \Rightarrow r = 12\text{ cm}$

2. $C = 2\pi r = 2 \times \pi \times 12$

Answer: $24\pi \text{ cm}$
