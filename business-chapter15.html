Key Concepts

01

Globalisation is the growing integration of the world’s economies - goods, services, people, money and ideas move more freely between countries.

02

Countries trade because of differing resources, climate, labour costs and skills: specialisation and trade raise total output and choice.

03

A multinational company (MNC) has operations in more than one country, locating production to reduce costs and reach markets.

04

An exchange rate is the price of one currency in terms of another; appreciation makes imports cheaper and exports dearer, depreciation the reverse.

05

MNCs bring jobs, skills and infrastructure to host countries, but can also repatriate profits, pressure local firms and cause environmental damage.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
GlobalisationThe increasing integration and interdependence of the world’s economies, with freer movement of goods, services, capital and people.
Multinational Company (MNC)A business with its headquarters in one country and operations (factories, shops or offices) in others.
Exchange RateThe price of one currency expressed in terms of another currency.
AppreciationA rise in the value of a currency - imports become cheaper, exports become more expensive abroad.
DepreciationA fall in the value of a currency - imports cost more, exports become cheaper for foreign buyers.
Import QuotaA physical limit on the quantity of a good that may be imported, protecting domestic producers.
03

Exam Tip

Exam tip

Exchange-rate questions always resolve to two effects: a stronger currency helps IMPORTERS and hurts EXPORTERS; a weaker currency helps EXPORTERS and hurts importers. Then link to profit: exporters earn more foreign currency, worth less at home after depreciation.

04

Practice Questions

Structured practice — write your answer, then reveal the model.

FOUNDATION

The domestic currency appreciates. State the effect on a business that imports raw materials and on a business that exports finished goods.

Model answerThe importer pays LESS in domestic currency for the same materials, so costs fall (1). The exporter’s goods become more expensive for foreign buyers, so sales may fall - or the same foreign earnings convert to LESS domestic currency (1).
CHALLENGE

Discuss two benefits and two drawbacks for a developing country of allowing a multinational car maker to build a factory there.

Model answerBenefits: employment and training for local workers (1); new technology and infrastructure, plus tax revenue for the government (1). Drawbacks: profits are repatriated to the home country rather than reinvested (1); local suppliers may be undercut and the country becomes dependent on one large employer (1).
