Key Concepts

01

Control accounts summarise the total of a ledger.

02

They help locate errors and prevent fraud.

03

The sales ledger control account shows total trade receivables.

04

The purchases ledger control account shows total trade payables.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Control accountAn account summarising all the individual accounts in a ledger.
Trade receivablesCustomers who owe the business money for credit sales.
Contra (set-off)Offsetting an amount owed by a customer against an amount owed to them as a supplier.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Discounts allowed and returns inwards go on the credit side of the sales ledger control account.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. State two benefits of control accounts.

Model answerThey help locate errors quickly and reduce the risk of fraud.
Application

2. Where are credit sales entered in the sales ledger control account?

Model answerOn the debit side.
