Key Concepts

01

Development is measured by indicators such as GDP per head and the Human Development Index (HDI).

02

Absolute poverty means being unable to afford basic needs.

03

Population growth depends on birth rates, death rates and net migration.

04

Developing countries often have young populations and large primary sectors.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Human Development IndexA measure of development combining income, education and life expectancy.
Absolute povertyA condition where income is too low to meet basic needs such as food and shelter.
Net migrationThe difference between the number of immigrants and emigrants.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Use HDI rather than GDP alone when comparing living standards, and explain why income is not the whole picture.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Why is GDP per head not a perfect measure of living standards?

Model answerIt ignores income distribution, the quality of life, the environment and unpaid work.
Application

2. Give two policies to reduce poverty.

Model answerProviding free education to improve skills and introducing a minimum wage.
