Volume of Cylinder

Pythagoras Theorem
a² + b² = c²
c = hypotenuse
Sine Ratio
sin(θ) = O / H

Cosine Ratio
cos(θ) = A / H
A = Adjacent, H = Hypotenuse
Tangent Ratio
tan(θ) = O / A

Quadratic Formula

Straight Line
y = mx + c
