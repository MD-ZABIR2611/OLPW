Bank Reconciliation

Agreeing the cash book with the bank statement.

04Key concepts
03Key terms
02Practice questions
—Your status
01

Key Concepts

01

Differences arise from timing and from items not yet recorded in the cash book.

02

Unpresented cheques and outstanding deposits are timing differences.

03

Bank charges and direct debits must be entered in the cash book first.

04

The bank reconciliation statement proves the updated cash book balance.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Unpresented chequeA cheque paid out and recorded in the cash book but not yet shown on the bank statement.
Outstanding lodgementA deposit recorded in the cash book but not yet credited by the bank.
Direct debitAn arrangement allowing a creditor to take payments directly from a bank account.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Update the cash book before preparing the reconciliation statement; examiners often award marks for both steps.

Practice Questions

1. Give two reasons why the cash book and bank statement may differ.

Model answerUnpresented cheques and bank charges not yet entered in the cash book.
Application

2. Where are bank charges first recorded when reconciling?

Model answerOn the credit side of the cash book, reducing the balance.
