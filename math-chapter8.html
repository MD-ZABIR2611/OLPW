SECTION 01 — CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Triangles & Directions

This chapter introduces the fundamental relationships between the sides and angles of right-angled triangles. You will learn Pythagoras' theorem for finding lengths, trigonometry (SOH CAH TOA) for finding angles and sides, and how to apply these to navigation and bearings.

SECTION 02 — LEARNING OBJECTIVES

Chapter Goals

  • Use Pythagoras' theorem to find missing sides.
  • Use SOH CAH TOA to find missing sides and angles.
  • Understand and apply the Sine and Cosine rules for non-right triangles.
  • Solve bearing problems using trigonometry.
SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Right-Angled Triangles

Pythagoras' Theorem: In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.

Trigonometry Ratios (SOH CAH TOA)

Sine (SOH): $\sin(\theta) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$

Cosine (CAH): $\cos(\theta) = \frac{\text{Adjacent}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$

Tangent (TOA): $\tan(\theta) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}}$

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

HYPOTENUSE: The longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.

BEARING: The angle measured in degrees clockwise from North. Always written as 3 digits (e.g., $045^\circ$).

ANGLE OF ELEVATION: The angle between the horizontal line of sight and an object above it.

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Finding a Missing Side (Trigonometry)

Find the length of the side marked $x$ in a right-angled triangle where the Hypotenuse is $12\text{ cm}$ and the angle is $30^\circ$. The side $x$ is opposite the angle.

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Choose ratio SOH ($\sin$) We have Opposite and Hypotenuse
2. Write formula $\sin(30^\circ) = \frac{x}{12}$ Substitute values
3. Rearrange $x = 12 \times \sin(30^\circ)$ Multiply by 12
4. Calculate $x = 12 \times 0.5 = 6$ Final answer
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Calculator Mode

Always check that your calculator is in DEGREES (DEG or D) mode, not RADIANS (RAD) or GRADIANS (GRA). If $\sin(30)$ gives $0.5$, you are in the correct mode. If it gives $0.45$, you are in radians.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Using Pythagoras to find an angle, or using Trigonometry to find a side without a right angle.

Correction: Pythagoras ONLY works for lengths in right-angled triangles. Trig requires an angle and a side.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

A ship sails $10\text{ km}$ on a bearing of $060^\circ$ from port $P$. How far East of $P$ is the ship? Give your answer to 1 decimal place.

3 marks:

1. Distance East = $10 \times \sin(60^\circ)$

2. $10 \times 0.866 = 8.66...$

Answer: $8.7\text{ km}$
