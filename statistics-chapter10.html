Key Concepts

01

Probability ranges from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

02

P(not A) = 1 − P(A).

03

For mutually exclusive events, P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).

04

For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B).

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
