Karak and Bibhakti

কারক ও বিভক্তি: case and inflection.

01

Key Concepts

01

Karak shows the relationship of nouns to the verb.

02

There are six karaks.

03

Bibhakti are endings added to words.

04

Example: কর্তৃকারক is the subject.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
কারকCase: relationship between nouns and verbs.
বিভক্তিInflectional endings added to words.
কর্তৃকারকNominative case: the doer of the action.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Ask 'who?', 'what?', 'by what?' questions to find the karak.

Practice Questions
