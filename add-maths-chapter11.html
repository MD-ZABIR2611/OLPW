Key Concepts

02

An arithmetic progression has a common difference d.

03

A geometric progression has a common ratio r.

04

A geometric series converges to a/(1 − r) when |r| < 1.

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Arithmetic progressionA sequence with a constant difference between terms.
Geometric progressionA sequence with a constant ratio between terms.
Sum to infinityThe limit of the sum of a convergent geometric series.
