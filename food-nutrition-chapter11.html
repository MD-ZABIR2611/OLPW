Key Concepts

01

Micro-organisms and enzymes cause spoilage.

02

Bacteria need warmth, moisture, food and time.

03

Preservation removes one or more conditions for growth.

04

Methods include freezing, drying, canning and pickling.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Micro-organismA tiny living thing such as bacteria, yeast or mould.
PasteurisationHeating to kill harmful bacteria.
Danger zoneThe temperature range 5°C to 63°C where bacteria multiply fastest.
