Cost structures, break-even analysis and quality control.

Key Concepts

01

Total cost = fixed cost + variable cost.

02

Break-even output = fixed costs / contribution per unit.

03

Margin of safety = actual output - break-even output.

04

Quality control inspects products; quality assurance builds quality into every stage.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Break-even pointThe level of output where total revenue equals total cost.
ContributionSelling price per unit minus variable cost per unit.
Quality assuranceA system that checks quality at every stage of production.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Fixed costs are $12,000, price $20 and variable cost $8. Calculate break-even output.

Model answerContribution = 20 - 8 = $12. Break-even = 12,000 / 12 = 1,000 units.
Application

2. State one advantage of quality assurance.

Model answerFaults are prevented early, reducing waste and costs.
