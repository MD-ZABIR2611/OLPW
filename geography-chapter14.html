Key Concepts

01

Non-renewable energy includes coal, oil and gas.

02

Renewable energy includes solar, wind and hydroelectric power.

03

Water supply depends on rainfall, rivers and groundwater.

04

Water shortages can be tackled with dams, desalination and conservation.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Renewable energyEnergy from sources that will not run out.
Hydroelectric powerElectricity generated by flowing water.
Water stressWhen demand for water exceeds the available supply.
