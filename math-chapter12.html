Circle Theorems & Advanced Geometry

SECTION 01 — CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Geometry of the Circle

Circle theorems describe the relationships between angles, radii, and chords within a circle. This chapter covers the fundamental theorems required for IGCSE/O-Level, teaching you how to find missing angles and prove geometric properties.

SECTION 02 — LEARNING OBJECTIVES

Chapter Goals

SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

The Fundamental Theorems

1. Angle at Center: The angle at the center is twice the angle at the circumference subtended by the same arc.

2. Angle in Semicircle: An angle in a semicircle is always $90^\circ$.

3. Same Segment: Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.

4. Cyclic Quadrilateral: Opposite angles sum to $180^\circ$.

5. Alternate Segment: The angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the alternate segment.

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

TANGENT: A straight line that touches a circle at exactly one point.

CHORD: A straight line joining two points on the circumference.

CYCLIC QUADRILATERAL: A 4-sided shape where all vertices lie on the circumference of a circle.

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Applying Circle Theorems

Find the value of $x$ in a cyclic quadrilateral where the opposite angles are $(3x)^\circ$ and $(2x + 30)^\circ$.

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Apply theorem $3x + 2x + 30 = 180$ Opposite angles sum to $180^\circ$
2. Simplify $5x + 30 = 180$ Combine like terms
3. Solve for $x$ $5x = 150 \Rightarrow x = 30$ Subtract 30, divide by 5
4. Final Answer $x = 30$ Check: $90 + 90 = 180$
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Geometric Proof

When solving geometry problems, always state the theorem you are using. E.g., "Angle A = $50^\circ$ because opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral sum to $180^\circ$." This guarantees method marks.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Assuming all triangles in a circle are isosceles.

Correction: Only triangles formed by two radii and a chord are isosceles. Check for the center point.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • Center angle = $2 \times$ circumference angle.
  • Semicircle angle = $90^\circ$.
  • Cyclic Quad: Opposite angles = $180^\circ$.
  • Tangent $\perp$ Radius ($90^\circ$).
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

In a circle, the angle at the center is $110^\circ$. Find the angle at the circumference subtended by the same arc.

1 mark:

Angle at circumference = $110^\circ / 2 = 55^\circ$.
