Clubs & Societies

Receipts and payments accounts, income and expenditure accounts, and the subscription adjustments clubs must make.

Key Concepts

01

A club is a not-for-profit organisation: instead of profit it calculates a SURPLUS (income over expenditure) or a DEFICIT.

02

The receipts and payments account is a summary of CASH received and paid during the year - it is not an income statement.

03

The income and expenditure account is the club’s version of an income statement: income EARNED minus expenses for the period (accruals basis).

04

Subscriptions must be adjusted: add subscriptions in arrears (owing) at the end, deduct subscriptions received in advance, and subtract arrears brought forward that were collected.

05

Life membership is spread over the expected membership period, and a bar trading account is prepared if the club sells refreshments.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Receipts and payments accountA summary of all cash received and paid by a club during the year, opening and closing with cash/bank balances.
Income and expenditure accountThe not-for-profit equivalent of an income statement, matching income earned with expenses incurred.
SurplusIncome earned exceeding expenses incurred - the not-for-profit equivalent of profit.
Subscriptions in arrearsSubscriptions members owe but have not yet paid; an asset, added to this year’s subscription income.
Subscriptions in advanceSubscriptions received this year but relating to next year; a liability, deducted from this year’s income.
Bar trading accountA mini trading account for a club bar showing bar gross profit, transferred to the income and expenditure account.
03

Exam Tip

Exam tip

Subscriptions adjustments follow one rule: the income and expenditure account shows subscriptions EARNED this year. Build the account: subscriptions received, add arrears at end, add advance at start, less advance at end, less arrears at start.

Practice Questions

Structured practice — write your answer, then reveal the model.

FOUNDATION

A club received $4,200 subscriptions during the year. Subscriptions owing at the start were $250 and at the end $400; subscriptions received in advance at the end were $150. Calculate the subscriptions income for the year.

Model answerSubscriptions income = received 4,200 + arrears at end 400 + advance at start 0 − advance at end 150 − arrears at start 250 (1) = $4,200 (1).
CHALLENGE

Explain why a club prepares a receipts and payments account AND an income and expenditure account, and state one item that appears in each but not the other.

Model answerThe receipts and payments account records CASH movements only (1). The income and expenditure account matches income EARNED with expenses INCURRED on the accruals basis (1). Example: subscriptions in arrears appear only in the income and expenditure account (a debt), while purchase of equipment appears only in receipts and payments (a capital item) (1).
