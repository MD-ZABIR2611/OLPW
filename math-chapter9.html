Vectors & Transformations

CORE
SECTION 01 — CHAPTER OVERVIEW

Movement & Space

Vectors describe movements in terms of distance and direction. This chapter covers vector arithmetic, calculating magnitude, and geometric transformations including translations, reflections, rotations, and enlargements.

SECTION 02 — LEARNING OBJECTIVES

Chapter Goals

  • Represent vectors in column form.
  • Add, subtract, and multiply vectors by a scalar.
  • Calculate the magnitude of a vector.
  • Describe and perform translations, reflections, rotations, and enlargements.
SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Vector Arithmetic

A vector has both size (magnitude) and direction. It is often written as a column vector $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$, where $x$ is the horizontal movement and $y$ is the vertical movement.

Transformations

Translation: Moving a shape. Described by a column vector $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$.

Reflection: Flipping a shape over a mirror line (e.g., $y=x$, $x=2$).

Rotation: Turning a shape around a center. Requires center, angle, and direction.

Enlargement: Resizing a shape by a scale factor from a center.

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

VECTOR: A quantity that has both magnitude (size) and direction.

MAGNITUDE: The length of a vector. Calculated using Pythagoras: $|\mathbf{v}| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$.

SCALAR: A quantity that has size only (a number).

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Magnitude of a Vector

Find the magnitude of the vector $\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Write formula $|\mathbf{v}| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ Pythagoras
2. Substitute $|\mathbf{v}| = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2}$ $x=4, y=3$
3. Calculate $|\mathbf{v}| = \sqrt{16 + 9} = \sqrt{25}$ Square and add
4. Final Answer $5$ Square root of 25
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Describing Transformations

If asked to "describe" a transformation, you must state the TYPE and the FULL DETAILS. For a reflection, state the mirror line equation. For a rotation, state center, angle, and direction (CW/CCW). For an enlargement, state scale factor and center.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Writing a translation vector as $(x, y)$ instead of a column vector.

Correction: Vectors must be written vertically: $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • Vector: $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$
  • Magnitude: $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$
  • Translation: Moves shape by vector.
  • Enlargement: Multiplies sides by scale factor.
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

Shape A is enlarged to form Shape B. If a side on Shape A is $4\text{ cm}$ and the corresponding side on Shape B is $12\text{ cm}$, what is the scale factor of the enlargement?

1 mark:

Scale factor = $\frac{\text{New}}{\text{Old}} = \frac{12}{4} = 3$
