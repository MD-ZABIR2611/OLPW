Key Concepts

01

Gradient m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁).

02

Parallel lines have equal gradients; perpendicular gradients multiply to −1.

03

Midpoint = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2).

04

Non-linear relationships can be converted to straight lines using logs.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
GradientThe steepness of a line: change in y over change in x.
Perpendicular bisectorA line at right angles through the midpoint of a segment.
Linear lawTransforming variables so a relationship plots as a straight line.

Exam Tip

Examiner says

For y = axⁿ, plot lg y against lg x: the gradient is n and the intercept is lg a.
