Key Concepts

01

Insurance pools risks so that the losses of a few are paid by the premiums of many.

02

Principles include utmost good faith, insurable interest, indemnity and contribution.

03

Common types are fire, marine, motor and life assurance.

04

Some risks, such as losses from poor management, cannot be insured.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
PremiumThe payment made to an insurance company for cover.
IndemnityThe principle that the insured is restored to the same financial position as before the loss.
Insurable interestThe insured must suffer a financial loss if the event occurs.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Learn each principle of insurance with a short example; they are regularly tested.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Explain the principle of utmost good faith.

Model answerBoth parties must disclose all relevant facts honestly when taking out insurance.
Application

2. Why can a business not insure against a fall in demand?

Model answerIt is a non-insurable risk because the probability cannot be calculated and it depends on business decisions.
