Key Concepts

01

In reverse percentage problems the original amount is found by dividing by the multiplier, for example 250 after a 25% increase means 250/1.25 = 200.

02

For similar figures, lengths scale by k, areas by k² and volumes by k³; a scale of 1:20 multiplies areas by 400 and volumes by 8000.

03

Unit conversions must be planned before calculating: 60 000 cm² = 6 m² because 1 m² = 10 000 cm², and 1000 cm³ = 1 litre.

04

Angle results come in families: angles on a line sum to 180°, angles in a triangle sum to 180°, and parallel lines give alternate, corresponding and co-interior angle facts.

05

In multi-step questions, write an intermediate value with its units at the end of each step so later stages can reuse it correctly.

06

Checking by estimation catches errors: an answer wildly different from a rough mental estimate usually signals a misplaced decimal point or scale factor.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
MultiplierThe single decimal number that scales a quantity in one step, such as 1.25 for a 25% increase or 0.85 for a 15% decrease.
Reverse percentageThe method of recovering an original amount by dividing the new amount by the multiplier, rather than subtracting the percentage.
DepreciationThe decrease in value of an asset over time, usually calculated by multiplying by a retention factor less than 1 each period.
Scale factorThe ratio of corresponding lengths in similar figures, multiplying every length of one figure to give the other.
Area scale factorThe square of the length scale factor, converting an area of one similar figure to the corresponding area of the other.
Volume scale factorThe cube of the length scale factor, converting a volume of one similar solid to the corresponding volume of the other.
Alternate anglesEqual angles formed on opposite sides of a transversal cutting a pair of parallel lines, in a Z-shaped arrangement.
Co-interior anglesAngles between two parallel lines on the same side of a transversal, summing to 180°.

Exam Tip

Exam tip

Underline each quantity in the question as you use it, square the length scale factor for area and cube it for volume, and convert all units before you begin.

04

Practice Questions

Structured practice — write your answer, then reveal the model.

FOUNDATION

After a 25% price increase, a phone costs 250 dollars. Find the original price, showing why subtracting 25% of 250 would be wrong.

Model answerThe multiplier for a 25% increase is 1.25, so original × 1.25 = 250 (1). Original price = 250/1.25 = 200 dollars (1). Subtracting 25% of 250 gives 187.50 dollars, which is wrong because the 25% was added to the original 200, not to 250, so the base of the percentage is different (1). Check: 200×1.25 = 250 confirms the answer (1).
CHALLENGE

A model yacht is built to scale 1:20 of the real yacht. The model sail has area 150 cm² and the model hull holds 0.5 litres. Find the real sail area in m² and the real hull capacity in litres, explaining the scale factors used.

Model answerLength scale factor is 20, so the area scale factor is 20² = 400 (1). Real sail area = 150×400 = 60 000 cm² = 60 000/10 000 = 6 m² (1). The volume scale factor is 20³ = 8000 (1). Real hull capacity = 0.5×8000 = 4000 litres (1). Areas scale by the square and volumes by the cube of the length scale factor because area covers two dimensions and volume covers three (1).
