SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Quadratics & Functions

A quadratic equation is in the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$. If it cannot be factorised, you must use the quadratic formula or completing the square.

Functions Notation

Composite Function: $fg(x)$ means do $g$ first, then put the result into $f$. $fg(x) = f(g(x))$.

Inverse Function: $f^{-1}(x)$ reverses the effect of $f(x)$. Swap $x$ and $y$, then make $y$ the subject.

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

QUADRATIC EQUATION: An equation of degree 2, containing an $x^2$ term as its highest power.

DISCRIMINANT: The part of the quadratic formula under the square root ($b^2 - 4ac$). Determines the number of roots.

SECTION 06 — CALCULATIONS & WORKED EXAMPLES

Quadratic Formula

Solve $2x^2 + 3x - 5 = 0$ using the formula.

STEP WORKING NOTES
1. Identify coefficients $a=2, b=3, c=-5$ From $ax^2 + bx + c$
2. Write formula $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ Standard form
3. Substitute $x = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{3^2 - 4(2)(-5)}}{2(2)}$ Insert values
4. Calculate discriminant $\sqrt{9 - (-40)} = \sqrt{49} = 7$ $9 + 40 = 49$
5. Final Answer $x = \frac{-3 \pm 7}{4} \Rightarrow x=1, x=-2.5$ Two solutions
SECTION 07 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Exact Answers

If a question asks for exact answers, leave your final result as a surd (e.g., $x = \frac{3 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$). Do not use a decimal approximation.

SECTION 08 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Doing $f$ first then $g$ when calculating $fg(x)$.

Correction: Work from right to left. $fg(x)$ means $g$ is the inner function applied first.

SECTION 09 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  • $fg(x) = f(g(x))$ (Right to left).
  • Inverse: Swap $x, y$, make $y$ subject.
  • Discriminant $>0$: 2 roots. $=0$: 1 root. $<0$: 0 roots.
SECTION 10 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Challenge)

If $f(x) = 2x - 5$, find $f^{-1}(x)$.

3 marks:

1. Let $y = 2x - 5$

2. Swap $x, y$: $x = 2y - 5 \Rightarrow x + 5 = 2y$

3. $y = \frac{x + 5}{2}$. So $f^{-1}(x) = \frac{x + 5}{2}$
