Key Concepts

01

Gross margin = gross profit / revenue x 100.

02

Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities.

03

Inventory turnover shows how quickly inventory is sold.

04

Ratios should be compared over time or with similar businesses.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
Current ratioCurrent assets divided by current liabilities, measuring the ability to pay short-term debts.
Profit marginProfit for the year as a percentage of revenue.
LiquidityThe ability of a business to meet its short-term obligations.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

After calculating a ratio, always comment on what it means and suggest a reason or improvement.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Current assets are $15,000 and current liabilities $10,000. Calculate the current ratio.

Model answer15,000 / 10,000 = 1.5 : 1.
Application

2. Give one way to improve the gross margin.

Model answerIncrease selling prices or find a cheaper supplier to reduce cost of sales.
