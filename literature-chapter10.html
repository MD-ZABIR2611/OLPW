Key Concepts

01

A tragic hero has a fatal flaw (hamartia).

02

Tragedy ends in suffering or death.

03

Shakespeare uses blank verse for noble characters and prose for lower-status ones.

04

Supernatural elements often create tension.

Key Terms

TermExam definition
HamartiaThe fatal flaw that leads to a tragic hero's downfall.
Blank verseUnrhymed iambic pentameter.
CatharsisThe release of emotion felt by the audience at the end of a tragedy.

Practice Questions
