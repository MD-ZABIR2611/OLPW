Definitions Database

Diffusion
The net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration, down a concentration gradient.
Chapter 02
Osmosis
The net movement of water molecules from a region of higher water potential to a region of lower water potential, through a partially permeable membrane.
Chapter 02
Active Transport
The movement of particles from a region of lower concentration to a region of higher concentration, against a concentration gradient, requiring energy from respiration.
Chapter 02
Enzyme
A biological catalyst, usually a protein, that speeds up a chemical reaction without being changed.
Chapter 04
Denaturation
The permanent change in the shape of an enzyme's active site, caused by high temperatures or extreme pH, preventing the substrate from binding.

Photosynthesis
The process by which plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, producing oxygen as a waste product.
Chapter 05
Transpiration
The evaporation of water from the leaves of a plant, creating a tension that pulls water up through the xylem.

Respiration
The chemical reaction in cells that breaks down glucose to release energy.
