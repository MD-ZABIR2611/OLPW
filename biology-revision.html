Final Revision Hub

Key Biological Processes

  • Photosynthesis: CO2 + Water → Glucose + Oxygen
  • Aerobic Respiration: Glucose + Oxygen → CO2 + Water
  • Anaerobic (Humans): Glucose → Lactic Acid
  • Anaerobic (Yeast): Glucose → Ethanol + CO2
  • Enzyme action: Lock and Key model
  • Reflex Arc: Stimulus → Receptor → Sensory → CNS → Motor → Effector
  • Homeostasis: Negative feedback loop (Insulin/Glucagon)
  • Natural Selection: Variation → Survival → Reproduction

Essential Calculations

  • Magnification = Image size ÷ Actual size
  • Percentage Change = (Change ÷ Original) × 100
  • Rate = Change ÷ Time
  • Population Estimate = (Mean per quadrat) × (Total area)

Must-Know Practicals

  • Potato Osmosis (calculating % change in mass)
  • Food Tests (Benedict's, Iodine, Biuret, Ethanol)
  • Effect of temperature on Amylase activity
  • Testing a leaf for starch
  • Potometer (measuring transpiration rate)
  • Quadrat sampling (estimating population size)

Exam Command Words

  • STATE: Give a short fact.
  • DESCRIBE: Say what happens/trends in data.
  • EXPLAIN: Give reasons/mechanisms (use "because").
  • COMPARE: Give similarities and differences.
  • CALCULATE: Show formula, working, and units.
