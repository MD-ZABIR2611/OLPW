Key Concepts

01

Badr (624 CE) was the first major Muslim victory.

02

At Uhud (625 CE) Muslims suffered setbacks when archers left their posts.

03

The Battle of the Trench (627 CE) defended Madinah.

04

The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (628 CE) led to peace.

TermExam definition
