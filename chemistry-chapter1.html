STATES OF MATTER
CURRICULUM BY KINETIC GOAL

SECTION 01
⬛

The Solid State

Maximize density. Particles are packed tightly in a rigid lattice structure, vibrating only in fixed positions. High structural integrity, incompressible.

  • Particle Movement Vibrational
  • Compressibility None
  • Density Level Maximum
  • Bond Strength Extreme
💧

The Liquid State

Fluid adaptation. Particles break their rigid bonds, allowing for flow and shape adaptation while maintaining constant volume. Built for versatility.

  • Particle Movement Translational
  • Compressibility Very Low
  • Density Level High
  • Bond Strength Moderate
💨

The Gaseous State

Total liberation. Particles move at high velocities, completely overcoming intermolecular forces. Maximum kinetic energy, infinite expansion.

  • Particle Movement Random/Free
  • Compressibility High
  • Density Level Low
  • Bond Strength Zero

FE / IRON

Head Strength Coach

Transition metal specialist. High atomic mass (55.85) and structural density. Expert in metallic bonding and load-bearing lattices.

  • High Melting Point
  • Ferromagnetic
  • Electron Config: [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s²

C / CARBON

Allotropic Conditioning

The ultimate adapter. Capable of forming diamond (max hardness) or graphite (lubrication). Master of covalent bond structures.

  • Atomic #: 06
  • Tetrahedral Geometry
  • 4 Valence Electrons
  • Electronegativity: 2.55

NE / NEON

High-Energy Specialist

Inert but illuminating. Operates at peak ionization energy without reacting. Excels in high-voltage, monatomic gas environments.

  • Atomic #: 10
  • Noble Gas
  • Full Octet
  • Zero Reactivity
