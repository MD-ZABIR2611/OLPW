Key Concepts

01

Countries specialise in goods they produce efficiently and trade for others.

02

Protectionism includes tariffs, quotas and subsidies to domestic producers.

03

A floating exchange rate is set by the demand for and supply of a currency.

04

The current account records trade in goods and services, plus income and transfers.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
TariffA tax placed on imported goods.
Exchange rateThe price of one currency in terms of another.
Current account deficitWhen a country's spending on imports exceeds its earnings from exports.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

For exchange-rate questions, remember SPICED: a Strong Pound (currency) makes Imports Cheaper and Exports Dearer.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. State two reasons for protectionism.

Model answerTo protect infant industries and to protect domestic jobs from foreign competition.
Application

2. Explain how a fall in a currency's value affects exports.

Model answerExports become cheaper for foreign buyers, so demand for exports is likely to rise.
