SECTION 03 — CORE THEORY

Memory & Storage

RAM (Random Access Memory) is volatile; it loses its data when power is turned off. It holds the current OS and running applications.

ROM (Read Only Memory) is non-volatile. It holds the BIOS (boot instructions) and cannot be written to by the user.

Secondary Storage

Magnetic (HDD): Uses spinning magnetic disks. High capacity, cheap, but slow and has moving parts.

Solid-State (SSD): Uses flash memory. Fast, durable, no moving parts, but expensive per GB.

Optical (CD/DVD): Uses lasers to read pits on a reflective surface. Cheap, portable, but low capacity.

SECTION 04 — KEY DEFINITIONS

Examination Definitions

VOLATILE: Memory that loses its contents when the power is turned off.

VIRTUAL MEMORY: A feature of the OS that uses secondary storage as temporary RAM when physical RAM is full.

IoT: Internet of Things. The network of physical objects embedded with sensors and software that connect to the internet to exchange data.

SECTION 06 — EXAM TECHNIQUE

Choosing Storage

If an exam asks you to recommend a storage device, always justify based on the scenario. E.g., "Use an SSD for a laptop because it is durable (no moving parts) and fast, allowing the OS to boot quickly."

SECTION 07 — COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid These Errors

Mistake: Saying SSDs are magnetic.

Correction: SSDs use electronic flash memory. HDDs are magnetic. SSDs have no moving parts.

SECTION 08 — QUICK REVISION

Fast Facts

  • RAM = Volatile. ROM = Non-volatile.
  • HDD = Magnetic, moving parts. SSD = Flash, no moving parts.
  • Virtual Memory prevents crashes when RAM is full, but is slow.
  • IoT connects everyday objects (fridges, thermostats) to the internet.
SECTION 09 — EXAM QUESTIONS

Test Your Knowledge

Question 1 (Standard)

A photographer needs to transfer large 4K video files between a camera and a laptop in the field. Recommend a storage medium and justify your choice.

2 marks: Recommend an SD card or SSD. Justification: They use solid-state flash memory, meaning they have no moving parts, making them durable for outdoor field work. They also have fast read/write speeds suitable for large video files.
