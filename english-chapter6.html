Narrative Writing

Structuring engaging stories.

Key Concepts

01

A narrative needs a clear structure: opening, development, climax and resolution.

02

Create believable characters through action, dialogue and thought.

03

Start in the middle of the action to hook the reader.

04

Control pace by slowing down key moments.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
ClimaxThe point of greatest tension in a story.
Narrative voiceThe perspective from which a story is told, such as first or third person.
FlashbackA scene that goes back in time from the main story.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Plan your ending before you start writing so the story has a purposeful structure.

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. What is the advantage of a first-person narrator?

Model answerIt gives direct access to the character's thoughts and feelings, creating closeness with the reader.
Application

2. How can dialogue improve a story?

Model answerIt reveals character, moves the plot forward and varies the pace.
