Key Concepts

01

Context includes the writer's life, era and society.

02

Social attitudes shape characters and themes.

03

Context should be linked to the text, not added as a history essay.

04

Modern readers may interpret texts differently.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
ContextThe historical, social and cultural background of a text.
PatriarchyA society where men hold most power.
ReceptionHow audiences respond to a text at different times.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Only use context that helps explain the writer's choices or the reader's response.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. Why is context useful when studying a text?

Model answerIt helps explain why characters behave as they do and what the writer was responding to.
Application

2. What is the risk of too much context?

Model answerThe essay drifts away from analysing the text itself.
