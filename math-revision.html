Key Formulae Reminders

  • Compound Interest: $A = P(1 + \frac{r}{100})^n$
  • Speed = Distance / Time
  • Area of Trapezium = $\frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$
  • Sine Rule: $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B}$
  • Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$

Calculator Rules

  • Ensure calculator is in DEGREES mode for trigonometry.
  • Use the $\pi$ button, not 3.14, for exact answers.
  • Don't round until the very last step of a calculation.
  • Check answers by estimating (e.g., $19.8 \times 4.1 \approx 80$).

Exam Command Words

  • Calculate: Show full working, including substitution.
  • Show that: You must prove the statement given.
  • Estimate: Round numbers first, then calculate.
  • Give exact answer: Leave in terms of $\pi$ or $\sqrt{2}$.

Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting to square the radius in $A = \pi r^2$.
  • Mixing up opposite and adjacent in trigonometry.
  • Using the wrong scale on a graph.
  • Dropping negative signs during algebraic expansion.

Final Mathematics Checklist

  • I have memorised all key formulae.
  • I know the difference between rounding and truncating.
  • I can calculate bounds and error intervals.
  • I can solve simultaneous equations and quadratics confidently.
  • I can apply SOH CAH TOA and Pythagoras accurately.
  • I always show my working clearly.
