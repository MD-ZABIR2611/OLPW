Key Concepts

01

Production methods include job, batch and flow production.

02

Productivity is output per worker per period.

03

Lean production aims to reduce waste.

04

Just-in-time keeps inventory to a minimum.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
ProductivityThe output produced per worker in a given time.
Batch productionProducing a quantity of identical products before switching to another product.
Just-in-timeAn inventory system where materials arrive just when they are needed.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Match the production method to the product: job production for bespoke items, flow for mass-produced goods.
