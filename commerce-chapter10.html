Key Concepts

01

Warehouses store goods until they are needed.

02

They allow seasonal goods to be available all year.

03

Bonded warehouses store imported goods until duty is paid.

04

Modern warehouses use computerised stock control.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
WarehouseA building where goods are stored before distribution or sale.
Bonded warehouseA warehouse where imported goods are kept until customs duty is paid.
Stock controlManaging inventory so that the right amount is available when needed.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Link warehousing to price stability: storing goods evens out supply and prevents price swings.

Practice Questions

Recall

1. State two functions of a warehouse.

Model answerStoring goods safely and keeping seasonal goods available all year.
Application

2. Why do importers use bonded warehouses?

Model answerThey can delay paying customs duty until the goods are sold or removed.
