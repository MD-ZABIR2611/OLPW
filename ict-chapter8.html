Key Concepts

01

Physical risks include trailing cables and overloaded sockets.

02

E-safety means protecting personal data online.

03

Threats include hacking, phishing, pharming and malware.

04

Security measures include firewalls, encryption and strong passwords.

02

Key Terms

TermExam definition
PhishingFraudulent emails that trick users into revealing personal data.
EncryptionScrambling data so it cannot be understood without a key.
FirewallSoftware or hardware that monitors and filters network traffic.
03

Exam Tip

Examiner says

Distinguish phishing (email), smishing (SMS) and pharming (malicious code redirecting to fake websites).

04

Practice Questions

Recall

1. What is phishing?

Model answerSending fake emails to trick people into revealing personal information.
Application

2. How does encryption protect data?

Model answerIntercepted data cannot be read without the decryption key.
